# Phase 0 — Project Definition

## 0.1 Problem Definition

### Problem

Venture capital due diligence requires investors and analysts to collect, review, and analyze information from multiple sources, including startup documents, financial data, market information, competitor data, and business metrics.

The existing process can be **time-consuming, repetitive, difficult to standardize, and prone to information being overlooked**. It can also be difficult for analysts to trace important conclusions back to the evidence that supports them.

### Problem to Be Solved

**VentureLens aims to assist investors in performing structured and evidence-grounded startup due diligence by organizing relevant information, analyzing documents and financial data, identifying potential risks and opportunities, and generating transparent investment insights.**

The platform will focus on **decision support rather than autonomous investment decisions**. The final investment decision will remain with the human investor.

### Core Principle

**Evidence → Analysis → Insight → Human Decision**

### Primary Problems Addressed

1. **Information overload** — Large amounts of startup information must be reviewed.
2. **Manual analysis** — Significant parts of the due-diligence process are repetitive.
3. **Inconsistent evaluation** — Different startups may be evaluated using different approaches.
4. **Hidden risks** — Important financial or business risks may be overlooked.
5. **Poor traceability** — Conclusions may not always be directly connected to supporting evidence.
6. **Time-consuming reporting** — Preparing structured investment assessments and investment memos requires substantial effort.

### Expected Value

VentureLens should help reduce the manual effort involved in due diligence while improving **consistency, transparency, evidence traceability, and analytical efficiency**.
